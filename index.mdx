---
title: "Introduction"
description: "Turn Italian fiscal receipts into electronic invoices and recover the VAT."
---

The Recivu API lets expense management platforms send the fiscal receipts (_scontrini_) their users collect to Recivu. Recivu negotiates with each merchant to have the receipt converted into an electronic invoice (_fattura elettronica_), so the company can recover the VAT (IVA) on the expense.

<Info>
  Base URL: `https://api.recivu.it/v1`. The same URL serves the sandbox and production: the API key you send decides which one you hit.
</Info>

## How an integration works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register the company">
    Call [`POST /company`](/api-reference/companies/register-a-new-company-to-recivu) for each customer of yours that wants to recover VAT. Pass a `webhook_url` to receive status updates, and store the `webhook_signing_secret` returned in the response.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register its employees">
    Call [`POST /employee`](/api-reference/employees/add-a-new-employee) for each person who submits receipts on behalf of the company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit receipts">
    Send each receipt with [`POST /receipt`](/api-reference/receipts/request-invoice-conversion-from-receipt): the image and the data you extracted from it. Keep the `receipt_id` you get back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the outcome">
    Recivu calls your [webhook](/webhooks) when the conversion completes, fails, is reverted or turns out to be a duplicate. You can also poll [`GET /receipts/{id}`](/api-reference/receipts/request-the-status-related-to-a-conversion-request) at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the invoice">
    Once a receipt is `completed`, download the invoice PDF with [`GET /invoices_download/{id}`](/api-reference/receipts/download-invoice-pdf).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Versioning

The API follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Breaking changes only ship in a new `MAJOR` version, with notice, so you can pin your integration to a `MAJOR` line. Every change is listed in the [changelog](/changelog), and breaking entries are tagged **BREAKING**.
